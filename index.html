About

I was born and raised in Rome, in Testaccio, and I still try to know everything about my city, from its music and cinema to its history.

My route into data was not a straight line. I chose a language high school because I wanted to understand how people live elsewhere, then studied Political Science and International Relations, which taught me to read institutions critically. What kept pulling at me was how information reaches people, and data turned out to be the answer. The MSc in Data Science and Management at LUISS gave me the technical side and a business lens.

I like work where models meet people: how news gets written, how the public reacts to a scandal, what a dataset says about an institution. I try to listen properly, not just wait for my turn to speak, and I think that matters as much in a project as in a conversation.

Away from work: travel, music and football.
