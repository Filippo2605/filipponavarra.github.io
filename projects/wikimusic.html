WikiMusic 2023: the musicians Wikipedia read most

A group project for the Data Visualization course at LUISS. We built our own dataset of the 1,000 musicians and bands most read on Wikipedia in 2023 and designed a dashboard to explore it.

Course
Data Visualization, LUISS
Team
Gian Lorenzo Marchioni, Marco Coci, Vincenzo Camerlengo, Filippo Navarra
Sources
Wikidata, Wikipedia API

Repository

Building the dataset

No ready-made dataset covered what we wanted, so we assembled one:

  1. From Wikidata, every person whose occupation is musician, singer, guitarist, songwriter or rapper, plus every entity that is a musical group, restricted to those active since 1960 and with at least one award.
  2. From the Wikipedia API, each candidate’s total page views in 2023. We kept the top 1,000.
  3. For those 1,000, we collected genre, nationality, birthplace with coordinates, the full article text, the links to other Wikipedia articles, the profile picture, and daily as well as yearly views.

Views

  • Where they come from: birthplaces on a map.
  • What they play: word clouds and a sunburst of genres and nationalities.
  • How they connect: a network of artists linked through their Wikipedia articles.
  • When people read them: views by genre over the year and a bar-chart race of the most-viewed artists day by day.
  • Who they are: a newspaper-style page combining each artist’s article introduction with the charts.

All the views were brought together in a single dashboard.
